Privacy Policy

Flashcard Catalog · Last updated 7 August 2026

Flashcard Catalog is a study app for making and reviewing flashcards. This policy explains what the app stores, what it sends elsewhere, and what control you have over it. The short version: your flashcards stay on your device unless you choose to sign in, and nothing is sent to an AI service unless you choose to import something with it.

What stays on your device

  • Your flashcards and folders — always saved on the device first, and usable with no account at all.
  • Pictures you attach to cards — stored only on the device that added them. They are never uploaded, not even when cloud sync is on.
  • Your AI provider API key, if you add one — kept in local storage on that device only. It is never uploaded to us, never synced to your account, and never shared.
  • Your appearance and provider settings.

If you sign in with Google

Signing in is optional and exists only to sync your cards between your own devices. If you do sign in:

  • Google provides the app with your email address, name, and an account identifier, handled through Firebase Authentication.
  • Your subjects, folders, card text, and review progress are stored in Google Cloud Firestore under your account identifier, so your other devices can read them.
  • Only you can read that data. It is not sold, shared, or used for advertising, and there is no analytics or tracking in the app.

You can sign out at any time, which stops syncing. To have the stored copy deleted, email the address at the bottom of this page and it will be removed.

AI-powered import

The app can turn a photo, PDF, Word document, or pasted text into flashcards. This only happens when you actively choose to import something.

  • On Android, photos are read on your device first using on-device text recognition. Only the recognised text is sent onward — usually not the photo itself.
  • The text (or, where on-device reading isn't possible, the image) is sent to an AI provider to generate the cards, and is handled under that provider's privacy policy. This happens one of two ways:
    • If you're signed in, your first few imports each day go through this app's own server, which forwards the content to Google Gemini and returns the cards. The server records only your account identifier and a count of how many imports you've made today, so the daily limit can be enforced. It does not store the content you import.
    • Beyond that daily limit, or if you've added your own API key, the content goes directly from your device to the provider you selected in Settings — Google Gemini or Anthropic Claude — using your own key.
  • Typing or pasting your own "Front | Back" cards involves no AI service and sends nothing anywhere.
Worth knowing: Google's free Gemini tier may use what you send — including imported text and photos — to improve their models. Anthropic's paid API does not train on your content. You choose the provider in Settings, and the app states this there as well. Don't import material you consider confidential under the free tier.

Children

The app is a general-audience study tool and is not directed at children under 13. It contains no advertising and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Deleting your data

  • On the device: deleting a subject or card removes it, and uninstalling the app removes everything stored locally, including any saved API key.
  • In the cloud: email the address below from the Google account you signed in with, and the synced copy will be deleted.
